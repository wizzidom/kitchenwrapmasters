Our Story

The Specialists Behind
Your Transformation.

Kitchen Wrap Masters is a professional vinyl wrapping company based in Johannesburg South, Gauteng — dedicated to transforming kitchens, fridges, and furniture without the cost or disruption of full replacement.

Who We Are

Vinyl Wrapping.
That's All We Do.

We are not a general home improvement company that offers wrapping as a side service. Vinyl wrapping is our sole focus — and that specialisation shows in the quality of every installation we complete.

Based in Johannesburg South, our team of 20 specialist installers serves clients across Gauteng and beyond. From a single fridge to a full kitchen transformation, we approach every project with the same attention to detail and commitment to quality.

Over the years, we have completed more than 259 projects — building a reputation for precision, reliability, and results that speak for themselves.

How We Work

Our Approach

Customer First

We take the time to understand your project and vision before recommending a solution. No rushed assessments, no upselling.

Transparent Pricing

You receive a clear quote before any work begins. The price you agree to is the price you pay. No hidden costs or surprise charges on the day.

Precision Installation

Every wrap is applied with care, from surface preparation through to the final edges and trims. We don't cut corners — literally or figuratively.

Guaranteed Materials

We use premium quality vinyl backed by a 3-year material guarantee. If the material fails under normal use, we stand behind it.

Local Knowledge

We're based in Johannesburg South. We know the area, we show up on time, and we're available when you need follow-up support.

Tidy Work Environment

We perform basic tidying after every installation. Your home is treated with respect throughout the process.
